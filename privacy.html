LAST BEACON - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

LAST BEACON ("the game", "we") is a mobile survival game published by HORVEX. This policy explains what data the game uses and why. We do not ask you to create an account with us, and we do not sell your data.

1. Data stored on your device

Your progress (days survived, unlocked perks, achievements, settings, purchases you have made) is saved in a file on your device. This file stays on your device unless you choose to sign in to Google Play Games, see section 3.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, and device and app interaction data to serve and measure ads. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), we show a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform before ads load, and you can change your choice later in Settings under "Privacy options". You can also reset or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings. See Google's advertising policy.

3. Google Play Games Services

If you sign in to Google Play Games, Google provides us with your Play Games player ID and display name so that achievements, leaderboards and optionally cloud save can work. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. Signing in is optional.

4. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We receive a purchase confirmation (product and receipt) to unlock what you bought. We never see your payment details.

5. Analytics and crash data

The game itself does not include its own analytics. Google Play may provide us with anonymous crash and performance reports collected by Android and Google Play.

6. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Sharing

We share data only with the service providers described above (Google AdMob, Google Play Games Services, Google Play Billing), and only as needed for those features to work.

8. Your choices and deleting your data

You can delete your data at any time in the game under Settings > Account > Delete my data. This erases your game progress from your device and from your Google Play Games cloud save. Leaderboard scores and achievements are stored by Google Play Games and are not erased by this; you can remove them in the Google Play Games app settings. To ask about other data held by Google (such as ads), use your Google account's privacy controls, or contact us at the address below.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above.
